## Threat Intelligence Frameworks

Threat intelligence frameworks provide structured models for describing adversary behavior, campaign progression, defensive countermeasures, and intelligence-sharing communities. They help analysts move from raw indicators to repeatable analysis by giving investigations a common vocabulary and a consistent way to map attacker activity.

These references are useful during alert triage, campaign tracking, threat hunting, detection engineering, and intelligence reporting.

### Objectives of Threat Intelligence Framework Analysis

* Map observed attacker behavior to recognized tactics, techniques, and procedures
* Understand how adversary campaigns progress across attack phases
* Structure intelligence reporting using shared analytical models
* Identify defensive opportunities and countermeasure mappings
* Support threat hunting, detection coverage reviews, and intelligence sharing

### Common Information Analyzed

* Tactics, techniques, and procedures observed in incidents
* Attack lifecycle phases and intrusion progression
* Defensive countermeasures and detection opportunities
* Sector-specific intelligence-sharing communities
* Relationships between adversaries, capabilities, infrastructure, and victims
