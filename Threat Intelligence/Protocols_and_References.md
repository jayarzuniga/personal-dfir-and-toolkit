## Threat Intelligence Protocols and References

Threat intelligence protocols and references define common formats, taxonomies, and vulnerability sources used to exchange and interpret security information. They help analysts normalize intelligence, assess vulnerability severity, and share findings clearly across teams and organizations.

These references are useful when producing intelligence reports, building enrichment pipelines, or validating vulnerability and sharing-context metadata.

### Objectives of Protocol and Reference Analysis

* Look up vulnerability identifiers and severity metrics
* Apply consistent information-sharing markings and handling guidance
* Understand structured intelligence exchange formats
* Normalize intelligence for platform ingestion and sharing
* Support vulnerability intelligence and threat reporting workflows

### Common Information Analyzed

* CVE identifiers and NVD vulnerability records
* CVSS scores and vulnerability severity metrics
* Traffic Light Protocol sharing markings
* STIX structured threat intelligence objects
* TAXII transport and exchange concepts
