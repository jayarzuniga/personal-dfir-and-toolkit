## Threat Intelligence Tools

Threat intelligence tools collect, enrich, correlate, and share indicators and contextual information about malicious activity. Analysts use these platforms to validate IP addresses, domains, URLs, hashes, malware samples, vulnerabilities, and observed attacker infrastructure.

These resources support IOC enrichment, campaign correlation, reputation checks, malware tracking, and intelligence sharing across security teams.

### Objectives of Threat Intelligence Tool Analysis

* Enrich indicators with reputation, ownership, and historical context
* Correlate IOCs with malware families, campaigns, and infrastructure
* Discover related domains, IPs, URLs, samples, and YARA matches
* Identify scanning, abuse, and malicious hosting patterns
* Support repeatable intelligence gathering and sharing workflows

### Common Information Analyzed

* IP, domain, URL, and file hash reputation
* Malware sample metadata and related indicators
* YARA rules and malware detection logic
* Passive intelligence feeds and community submissions
* Threat intelligence platform events and shared collections
