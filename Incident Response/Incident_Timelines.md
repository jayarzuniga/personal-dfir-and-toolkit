## Incident Timelines

Incident timelines organize forensic artifacts, logs, and analyst observations into a chronological view of attacker and system activity. Timelines help responders reconstruct what happened, identify initial access and lateral movement, and communicate the sequence of events clearly.

These tools and references are useful for post-compromise analysis, executive reporting, and validating containment scope.

### Objectives of Incident Timeline Analysis

* Reconstruct attacker activity across systems and data sources
* Identify initial access, persistence, lateral movement, and impact events
* Correlate forensic artifacts with logs and analyst observations
* Support incident reporting and lessons learned
* Validate scope, dwell time, and containment completeness

### Common Information Analyzed

* Filesystem timestamps and metadata
* Windows event logs and endpoint telemetry
* Registry, browser, shortcut, prefetch, and shell artifacts
* Analyst notes and response actions
* Cross-host event correlation and sequence reconstruction
