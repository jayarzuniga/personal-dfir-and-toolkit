## Incident Response Playbooks

Incident response playbooks provide repeatable procedures for handling common incident types and threat scenarios. They help teams move from alert to action using predefined investigation steps, containment options, evidence requirements, and communication checkpoints.

These resources are useful for building internal response procedures, tabletop exercises, SOC workflows, and threat hunting runbooks.

### Objectives of Incident Response Playbook Analysis

* Standardize response steps for common incident scenarios
* Reduce decision latency during high-pressure investigations
* Define evidence collection and containment actions
* Align SOC triage, escalation, and response workflows
* Support tabletop exercises and response maturity improvements

### Common Information Analyzed

* Incident type-specific investigation steps
* Containment, eradication, and recovery actions
* Evidence and artifact collection requirements
* Detection logic, hunting queries, and adversary tradecraft
* Communication and escalation checkpoints
